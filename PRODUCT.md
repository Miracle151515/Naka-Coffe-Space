# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static three-page HTML (`index.html`, `menu.html`, `about.html`), CSS, and JavaScript. Code-first build. No framework, build step, or backend.

## Users

Local cafe visitors and prospective visitors in Indonesia who want to feel NAKA's atmosphere, browse its indoor and outdoor spaces visually, scan the full coffee, cold brew, signature drink, snack, Nusantara, donburi, and ramen menu before visiting, check its single-outlet Ciracas facilities, location, and daily opening hours (`09:00 - 23:00`), and reach NAKA directly on WhatsApp or social media.

## Product Purpose

NAKA Coffee and Space is an Indonesian coffee shop in Ciracas, East Jakarta. This three-page company profile and browse-only menu showcase opens Home with a tall full-viewport architectural photograph and About with a full-bleed courtyard photograph followed immediately by minimalist Cream editorial intro bands (with Home's `.hero-intro` carrying the concise brand introduction and `Read About NAKA` link), previews NAKA's real food and beverage menu directly on Home (`#menu-preview`) with deep links into the two-part browse-only Menu Showcase (`menu.html`: a zero-scroll 2-column Split Studio Showcase above an interactive animated 10-page editorial Menu Book), equips visitors with a dominant Terracotta `Plan Your Visit` & `Outlet Facilities` band (`#visit-info`, `.section--visit`) tailored to NAKA's single Ciracas outlet, pairs About with a clean Material Character 4-swatch board and single-photo courtyard story split, presents verified Ciracas address, daily opening hours (`09:00 - 23:00`), and a live Google Maps embed in Find Us, and integrates direct WhatsApp contact, Google Maps location, and social links into a shared flat solid Terracotta Ink footer.

## Positioning

The site is built around the physical space, its official NAKA emblem, its real coffee and kitchen menu, and its four architectural materials: dominant burnt terracotta walls, matte black steel structure, poured concrete, and open-air tropical planting. Charcoal and Cream remain the primary structural grounds across the visual-first intros, space mosaic, Home Menu Showcase Teaser, Menu Showcase page, Material Character board, and location band, while calibrated Terracotta unmistakably owns NAKA's Home Plan Your Visit band (`#visit-info`), unscrolled navbar, and clean flat solid Terracotta Ink footer.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on mobile or desktop, often from social media or a Google Maps search. They scan visually for atmosphere, space, menu offerings and prices, location, daily hours (`09:00 - 23:00`), and direct WhatsApp contact. The site uses NAKA's official supplied SVG logo lockups, verified Ciracas location and live Google Maps embed, verified daily opening hours, and the complete 8-category menu (**68 main items + 4 Coffee Add-Ons**) with all 9 real NAKA 32-bit RGBA product specimen visuals extracted from the official 10-page Heyzine flipbook (`https://heyzine.com/flip-book/d3431d27da.html`), alongside owner-approved architectural concept space visuals, dummy WhatsApp numbers, and pending social links clearly disclosed until official assets and accounts are confirmed.

## Product Principles

- Atmosphere first: full-bleed space visuals lead Home and About with minimal text friction, while Menu (`menu.html`) opens directly into a zero-scroll 2-column Split Studio Showcase with instant 8-category icon switching above the fold, followed by the full animated 10-page editorial Menu Book.
- Browse-only clarity: Menu Showcase (`menu.html`) and Home's Menu Showcase Teaser (`#menu-preview`) present real NAKA drinks and food with clean tabular pricing and authentic product photography, free of e-commerce cart clutter.
- Honest by default: concept space imagery, dummy WhatsApp numbers, pending social links, and pending business story are explicitly disclosed, while confirmed menu, hours, logo, and Ciracas location are presented accurately.
- Direct contact: Customer Service and Bulk & Event Order open WhatsApp in one tap from the footer.
- Mobile is the primary visit: touch targets `>= 44px`, zero horizontal overflow from `320px` up.
