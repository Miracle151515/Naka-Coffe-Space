## Visitor mode

Persuade. The visitor is deciding whether NAKA is worth a visit.

## Audience and job

Local café visitors and prospective visitors in Indonesia arriving from social media or search. They scan atmosphere first, browse the space visually, preview the menu, check opening hours and single-outlet facilities, locate the café in Ciracas, and message NAKA directly on WhatsApp or open social channels.

## Action

Primary: explore the physical space (`#our-space`, interactive filter + photo lightbox) and preview the menu (`#menu-preview` -> `menu.html`). Secondary: read NAKA's story (`about.html` via `Read About NAKA` in `.hero-intro`), review opening hours and outlet facilities (`#visit-info`), or find the location (`#location`). Final: tap either direct WhatsApp contact row (Customer Service or Bulk & Event Order) in the Contact column to open WhatsApp directly, or open the circular Instagram/TikTok buttons once official URLs exist.

## Proof and content

Official NAKA Coffee & Space SVG logo assets supplied (`assets/images/naka-mark.svg`, `assets/images/naka-logo-vertical.svg`, `assets/images/favicon.svg`, plus inline SVG lockups). Owner approved generated architectural concept photography reflecting NAKA's terracotta, black steel, concrete, and planting palette, clearly labelled as concept visuals pending official shoot. Dummy WhatsApp numbers (`+62 812 0000 0001` and `+62 812 0000 0002`) link to `wa.me` with explicit prototype labels; Instagram, TikTok, and Google Maps open an honest placeholder dialog until official URLs are confirmed.
