## Visitor mode

Persuade. The visitor is deciding whether NAKA is worth a visit.

## Audience and job

Local café visitors and prospective visitors in Indonesia arriving from social media or search. They scan atmosphere first, browse the space visually, absorb NAKA's terracotta brand identity and story, locate the café, and message NAKA directly on WhatsApp or open social channels.

## Action

Primary: explore the physical space (`#our-space`, interactive filter + photo lightbox). Secondary: step into NAKA's story (`about.html` via the Terracotta Brand Profile band) or find the location (`#location`). Final: tap either direct WhatsApp contact row (Customer Service or Bulk & Event Order) in the Contact column to open WhatsApp directly, or open the circular Instagram/TikTok buttons once official URLs exist.

Dummy WhatsApp numbers (`+62 812 0000 0001` and `+62 812 0000 0002`) link to `wa.me` with explicit prototype labels; Instagram, TikTok, and Google Maps open an honest placeholder dialog until official URLs are confirmed.
