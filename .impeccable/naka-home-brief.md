# NAKA Coffee and Space, homepage

## Scope

Single-page company profile for desktop and mobile. Sections: navigation, hero, about, space, gallery, location, contact, footer. No other routes.

## Visitor mode

Persuade. The visitor is deciding whether NAKA is worth a visit.

## Audience and job

Local café visitors and prospective visitors in Indonesia, often arriving from social media or a maps search. They need to understand the atmosphere, find the café, and reach its social channels.

## Action

Primary: see the space (scroll to the Our Space section). Secondary: find the location (scroll to Find Us). Final: open Instagram or WhatsApp once official links exist. In this prototype those actions open an honest placeholder dialog.

## Proof and content

No approved business copy, photographs, address, map, social links, WhatsApp number, or logo file. The prototype uses the supplied `design.md` direction and clearly labelled placeholders. It must not invent business history, menu, prices, hours, address, facilities, or testimonials.

## Constraints

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Light editorial page with dark sections, no theme toggle. Text must meet WCAG AA. Every control needs a real behavior. Official actions use a placeholder dialog until URLs exist. No horizontal overflow from 320px up. Touch targets 44px minimum.

## Chosen direction

Pinned by `design.md`, "Modern Industrial Coffee Brand": terracotta wall surfaces, charcoal steel frames, cream paper, beige concrete, restrained olive, editorial serif display, grotesk body, squared controls. The lack of photography becomes a deliberate material register rather than a generic image placeholder.

## Memorable moment

The hero behaves like a facade: a tall terracotta panel is framed by charcoal steel while the oversized NAKA wordmark anchors the opposite side. The page keeps repeating that frame as a wayfinding motif across space, gallery, map placeholder, and contact actions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Treat NAKA's materials as the brand and refuse the generic café photo-collage, using a typographic facade and honest, designed placeholder blocks instead.

OWN-WORLD: Terracotta wall fields, charcoal steel frames, cream paper, beige concrete, hairline rules, squared controls, a high-contrast editorial serif display, a plain grotesk body, and olive only where planting appears.

STORY: The visitor understands that NAKA is an open-air coffee space in Indonesia, sees how the space is organised, finds the location, and knows how to reach the official channels once they are supplied.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed charcoal hero, a tall terracotta panel at right framed like a steel opening, a huge cream NAKA display wordmark at left with "Coffee and Space" beneath, a bilingual tagline and two squared CTAs at lower left. The primary action is "See the Space".

FORM: Pinned whole by `design.md`. seed: none (pinned), "Modern Industrial Coffee Brand" with the structure, palette, tone, and responsive brief specified in `design.md`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Unresolved decisions

Official photographs, address, Google Maps destination, Instagram URL, WhatsApp number, official description, and logo file. All remain labelled placeholders. Any replacement must come from NAKA, not from a stock image.
